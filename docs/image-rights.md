Considered and left out: George Wiedemann Brewery Complex, Newport KY (HABS KY-177). Its photographs were made by a
private contractor (Historic Preservation Associates), so the government-work basis does not clearly apply.

Captions quote the archive's own title, note or survey caption verbatim and say where and when the photo was taken.
People in the Pedal Wagon photo are not named and are shown as illustration of a brewery outing, not as endorsing
the site. North Country Brewing Co. is linked as a directory listing, not presented as a sponsor.
